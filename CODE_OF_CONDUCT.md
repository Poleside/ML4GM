# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, or sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit, or
reject contributions that are not aligned with this Code. They will take fair
corrective action and communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in all community spaces and when an individual officially
represents the community in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the
repository maintainers using the contact methods on their GitHub profiles. Do
not open a public issue. Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly,
avoid conflicts of interest, and protect the reporter's privacy and security.

## Enforcement Guidelines

### 1. Correction

For inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior, maintainers may
issue a private written warning and request a public apology.

### 2. Warning

For a violation through one incident or a series of actions, maintainers may
set consequences for continued behavior and require no interaction with the
people involved for a defined period.

### 3. Temporary Ban

For a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior, maintainers may
temporarily prohibit community interaction and public communication.

### 4. Permanent Ban

For a pattern of violations, harassment, aggression, or disparagement of groups,
maintainers may permanently prohibit public community interaction.
