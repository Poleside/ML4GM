| --- | --- | --- |

These roles record the public repository relationship and the authorization
confirmed for the Apache-2.0 open-source transformation. They do not imply
employment, institutional endorsement, or ownership of third-party datasets.

## Shared responsibilities

Maintainers share responsibility for:

- issue triage and contributor guidance;
- scientific and code review;
- continuous-integration health;
- documentation and data-provenance review;
- security-report handling;
- versioning, release notes, and release approval.

Changes to scientific claims, validation invariants, data rights, security
policy, or releases require maintainer review. Contributors may earn additional
responsibility through sustained, technically sound, and respectful work.
